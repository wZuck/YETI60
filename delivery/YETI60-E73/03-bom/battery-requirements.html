YETI60-BAT-1600-1S supplier drawing and acceptance specification

This is a review/RFQ specification for one custom protected single-cell pack per keyboard. No supplier has accepted it and no order is authorized. The assembly specification, interconnect specification, power calculation and battery study are its controlled inputs.

Cell and complete package

Reference cell: Honcell HCP404074, sheet dated 2024-04-01, 3.7 V nominal, minimum 1600 mAh/typical 1620 mAh under its stated test protocol. It is a cell reference, not an accepted finished pack. Its 0.2C=270 mA and 1C=1350 mA current rows conflict with the capacity rating. Require a corrected manufacturer sheet or signed clarification before procurement; do not reinterpret these numbers as confirmed 320/1600 mA limits. Manufacturer sheet

The entire finished pack must remain within 85 x 43 x 4.8 mm, including PCM, thermal protection, thermistor, weld/tab folds, wrap, insulation, adhesives, strain relief and the specified life/growth condition. The source cell's delivered/cycled thickness allowances must be included, not added later. Confirm full length including tabs and fold geometry; the bare-cell nominal outline is not the finished-pack envelope. Provide an orthographic dimensioned drawing with tolerances and sections through the thickest protection/tab/sensor areas, usable removal surfaces, mass and wire-exit datums.

The nominal pack bounds in the case are [228.5,-149,-9.4] to [313.5,-106,-4.6] mm. Do not scale the enclosure or clamp the pouch. Nominal floor-to-pack space is 0.20 mm in total; retention tape/insulation/assembly allowance must share it. The current pocket already belongs to the canonical bottom shell.

The independent protector and BQ24074 feedback need separate sensing arrangements. Do not connect a protector divider to the BQ thermistor node without a validated electrical design: its biasing can change the measured temperature. Voltage/current-only PCM and two-wire packs do not meet the temperature requirement. The charger IC's die thermal regulation is not cell thermal protection. Leads and assembly interface

Use one SHR-03V-S housing with three SSH-003T-P0.2-H contacts, AWG28 stranded copper, insulation OD <=0.65 mm. Pin 1 is red PACK+, pin 2 black PACK-, pin 3 yellow NTC to PACK-. Supply a drawing showing pin numbers and the latch/viewing direction; wire colors alone are insufficient. The pack leads/housing/contacts are included in the pack quantity, not additional loose purchases. JST SH drawing

Pack local origin is its minimum X/Y/Z corner. Required strain-relief lead-exit datum is [0,14,4.5] mm, outward along -X, with pin endpoints offset by +2/0/-2 mm in Y. This is an engineering interface request requiring supplier confirmation. The body and strain relief must meet the complete-pack envelope; the external insulated pigtail is separately screened in the case at this exit datum. Show both envelopes on the drawing. Use the per-wire free lengths and case-frame paths in the current E73 conductor table; they are insulation lengths between exit planes, not crimp cut lengths. Nominal free-length tolerance is +/-0.5 mm; the harness must additionally be preformed and retained within the controlled route. Length alone does not guarantee fit. Supplier must approve the chosen >=4 mm static bend radius and a >=5 mm pack lead transition zone without bending cell tabs or stressing welds.

Qualification sequence

Inspect the signed drawing, polarity, NTC resistance and complete envelope before connection. Exercise the charger with a battery simulator first. On qualified packs, record full CC/CV/termination and timer behavior with board-on configured USB500 and separately with board-off USB100, across accepted source voltage, system load and temperature conditions. Verify independent hot/cold interruption/recovery and closed-case charger/cell temperatures. Record switch-off leakage, capacity and normal-firmware battery-side current with USB/debugger disconnected. First-article testing and acceptance remain open; calculation is not a substitute.

This root specification applies to E73 CORNER-06 and its SH3 battery interface. The separate Raytac variant retains its own supplier document and wiring datums. Final E73 cable retention and supplier acceptance remain open.
