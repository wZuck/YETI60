CORNER-07 first article

Start with the bare board and the already-made C5 daughterboard. The bench stages do not require a case. Use a battery simulator for the first charging tests. The later stages require the NiZ mechanism, the specified pack and the closed case.

Copy this page for each unit. Record the PCB and firmware digests from the delivery manifest, the actual PCB thickness, the C5 revision, the pack identifier, ambient temperature, host, cable and instruments. Record measurements beside each result. A checked design report cannot populate a physical result.

Stage Test and evidence Pass condition
Factory CAM Compare the board preview and proposed stack with the order instructions. Inspect non-plated holes, slots, both stencil layers, drill locations at C42, C48 and D2, and aperture registration at C42, SW1, U5 and U6 The factory accepts the geometry and ordinary four-layer process. Record any proposed CAM changes before accepting them
Bare board Check joints and polarity, then use the staged limited-power test. Confirm the 3.3 V rail and the J3 contact map No short, abnormal current or heating. Correct rail and SWD identification. Soldering steps
Flash and open bench Program the calibrator using SWD. Start with the pack disconnected. Check the C5 cable orientation and USB enumeration Programmer verifies the image. Correct USB device enumerates at full speed and stays connected with the intended cable. USB path and signal checks Firmware and J3 pin map
Pack simulator Observe charger IN, OUT, BAT, current and VBUS at the E73 during USB preconfiguration, configuration, weak input, suspend, resume and board-off USB100. Check new and worn cable conditions as applicable Charging follows the documented policy. The whole-device 100/500 mA and hot-plug checks pass. Keyboard stays on USB HID during current foldback. VBUS at U1 stays within its operating range while active. No reset or loss of the typed sequence. Bench connections and test points
Plate and stabilizer fit Trial the received NiZ housing and stabilizer pairs with the 1U, 2.25U and 6U cuts. Record finished thickness, clips, cap socket and wire fit. Trial-fit coupon Both Enter and left Shift caps fit the short pair. The centered spacebar fits the long pair. Through the stroke there is no rubbing, clip lift or cap side load
All keys Assemble the plate, domes and springs outside the case. Save 60 calibration positions and program the matching normal image with settings retained. Test each press and release, plus both stabilized ends of the three large keys All 60 physical positions produce their expected key or layer behavior. Both edges of each stabilized key return without sticking. Mechanism guide
Physical battery Review the pack supplier drawing, ratings, polarity, thermistor and separately implemented temperature interruption. Measure charge current, termination, temperature and off-state draw after the simulator test Meets the complete supplier-confirmed pack specification, including independent temperature interruption and full charge timing
M05 dry-fit and reopening Record PCB thickness and cover seating. Follow the M05 first-article guide with matched wires, temporary retainers, an inert gauge, two tools and both parking supports. Release J2 then J4, verify the 25 mm vertical lift and reverse the procedure No forced motion, pinched leads, contact damage, loose plugs or load on the gauge. Record actual grip, wire feed and repeated service
Closed case Repeat USB typing during charging and BLE typing on battery after detach. Test orientations and normal hand positions at 2 m and 3 m. Measure charger and pack temperature. Bluetooth test and log Meets the target use range and pack/board temperature limits. No unexpected HID events, link loss or unsafe heating over the recorded duration and conditions

Record a failed stage before continuing. Do not qualify the finished build until the relevant failures are corrected and retested.
