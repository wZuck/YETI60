CORNER-07 revises the U5, U6, C42 and SW1 openings and relocates the D2 ground via. It carries forward the USB supply paths and the C42, C48 and SW1 ground via locations from CORNER-05 and CORNER-06. The native PCB, firmware, BOM and CAM in this delivery identify this revision. The current path preserves direct USB HID while charging after host configuration.

The four M05 AWG28 supply and return leads span 1.09527 m with nominal copper area 0.0804 mm², for 0.23486 Ω at 20 °C. Current drawn from the USB host

The 500 mA descriptor applies at the host port. After F1, U5.13 and U1.27 both connect to USB_5V, so upstream current includes the charger's input and the E73 USB PHY branch. In USB500 mode the BQ24074 input limit is 450 / 475 / 500 mA. Nordic specifies 2.4 mA typical for the active nRF52840 VBUS branch and supplies no maximum in that table. Combining the charger's upper limit with just that typical value gives 502.4 mA. This identifies a gap in the tolerance analysis. The board reaches that combined current only if the charger is loaded to its upper limit. USB-IF host-port policy · TI §8.5 · Nordic USBD current table

For a planning point, allocate 40 mA at U5 OUT, 2 mA for U5 housekeeping and 10 mA for the rest of the port current. With R40 at 2.21 kΩ, the upper programmed charge current gives 445.63 + 40 + 2 + 10 = 497.63 mA. The 2.37 mA remainder depends on the three assumed loads. In USB100 the 100 mA maximum likewise applies at U5 IN. During hot plug the directly connected mainboard capacitances C32 (4.7 µF) and C40 (2.2 µF) alone give the nominal estimate (4.7 + 2.2) × 5 = 34.5 µC. Test the whole device against the attach and steady-current criteria. Source-bound calculations · USB-IF inrush test, §B.4

If the configured host current fails, a trial 2.49 kΩ ±1% 0402 at R40 is the first component-value change to evaluate. It reduces the programmed maximum to 395.52 mA. The same allocations give 447.52 mA and 52.48 mA of modeled margin. This leaves the BQ24074 in USB500 with its input-voltage foldback. Before adopting the value, repeat USB and charge tests, including constant-voltage taper, termination, the safety timer and full-charge time.
