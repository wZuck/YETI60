# P2 design checkpoint

This is an engineering prototype design, not a manufacturing release. No physical board exists. The authorized milestone is a complete design and buildable firmware; physical EC calibration and manufacturing qualification follow samples.

- **Daughterboard:** retain Unified Daughterboard **C5 JSH**, pinned `2b6c52a`, its four M2 positions and J1's VBUS/D−/D+/GND mapping. Do not substitute UDB S.
- **Battery:** default custom **1600 mAh**, full envelope **85×43×4.8 mm**, based on HCP404074. It is the largest found in a stated 651-model, 1 mm-grid screening. Exact selected geometry has zero checked overlaps, 0.70 mm nominal fastener clearance and 3.6585 mm minimum wall after the 0.30 mm allowance. The canonical bottom includes the recess; FELK60 exterior union remains 325.700×125.200×31.190 mm.
- **Power:** retain BQ24074; R40=2.21k, R55=69.8k, R56=1.69k, all 1%. Programmed charge is 357.06/402.71/445.63 mA min/typ/max; fast timer is 6.9102/9.3067/11.7497 h; maximum termination current is 29.26 mA. These calculations do not certify complete charging. The factory pack requires independent charge-temperature interruption inside actual cell 15…43°C, separate NTC and supplier-signed protection/dimensions. The manufacturer's current-row inconsistency must be resolved before procurement release.
- **PCB:** ordinary JLC four-layer FR-4, JLC04161H-7628, nominal 1.6 mm (CAD envelope 1.6062 mm). No blind/buried/laser vias or module vias-in-pad; no fill/capping requirement. EC electrode copper, outline and mounting centers are retained. The sensing dielectric is 0.2104 mm; physical calibration remains required. Native DRC/parity and 270 physical pin-pair checks govern the current files.
- **Firmware:** normal and USB-CDC calibrator ARM ELF/HEX build from frozen ZMK/Zephyr dependencies. Both linked USB descriptors request 500 mA. Only an accepted host configuration enables USB500 charging; suspend disables charging and resume retains the accepted configuration. HEX checks protect the settings partition. USB/BLE HID, bonding and profiles are supplied by ZMK; on-device behavior remains untested.
- **Evidence:** current engineering drawings, PCB stills/animation and studio assembly renders have source/output manifests. The battery/caps/housings/material finishes remain design envelopes or illustrations. The [engineering evidence](audit/yeti60-engineering.json) and [integration](audit/integration.json) must pass against current hashes.

The [power model](engineering/power-budget.md) gives 90.77 h at its planning assumptions, not measured runtime. Supplier pack/current/thermal approval, final harness/plug serviceability, donor switch/dome stack, ordinary PCB acceptance/part ratings, resin/insert strength and first-article fit/charging remain acceptance gates. SI/RF screening has started; antenna layout blockers remain open. Current design details are in [materials](engineering/materials.md), [assembly](engineering/assembly.md) and [battery selection](engineering/battery-study.md); the comprehensive review records current checks and acceptance limits.
