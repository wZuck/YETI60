# YETI60 materials and procurement requirements

Revision P2, 2026-10-29. This table is a prototype procurement plan; rows with unqualified dimensions or candidate MPNs are not approved purchase orders. Quantities are per keyboard, without spares. Electronics use the current schematic, not a historical EC60 BOM.

## Electronics

Use the [inventory allocation](inventory-allocation.md) and [stock-adjusted mainboard CSV](../../hardware/bom/mainboard-purchase.csv) before buying loose parts. The current order-derived snapshot plans 10 mainboard parts from received stock and 66 to buy; actual remaining stock and capacitor qualification require confirmation. All other preparation quantities below remain applicable.

The [grouped mainboard CSV](../../hardware/bom/mainboard.csv) is regenerated by `python3 scripts/export_yeti60_bom.py`: **76 fitted components**, one DNP gain-adjustment site JP1, and no purchasable electrode, mounting-hole or SWD-pad parts. JP1 must remain open; R16/R17 already provide gain 101. There are 74 alternate-layout electrode footprints but **60 installed key mechanisms** in this HHKB plate. J3 is a six-pad SWD probe interface, not a soldered header.

The main J1 footprint has an inherited 1.0T-4P supplier identifier. JST SM04B-SRSS-TB is a candidate with a matching nominal interface; check retention pads and mating orientation against the [JST SH drawing](https://www.jst-mfg.com/product/pdf/eng/eSH.pdf) before ordering. ## Cable guides and auxiliary materials

Also prepare two rear bases, two removable bridges and one front base; 0.05 mm removable nonconductive double-sided tape and three retention films with total thickness <=0.10 mm. ## Tools and assembly release

Use an SWD probe plus the custom J3 pogo fixture, multimeter for every harness conductor/polarity, small torque-controlled driver, calipers and an insulated tool for SW100 access. No soldered per-key diodes, MX sockets or second MCU belong in this design. Quantities do not imply supplier availability or prices.

FELK60 supplies the plate/gasket mounting basis. [DELTA OMEGA BOM](https://github.com/wZuck/delta-omega/blob/main/docs/BOM.md) was reviewed at commit `64db818`: its separate mechanical/electronic rows and explicit cap envelope are useful documentation references; its MX switches, XIAO controllers and two batteries are not compatible substitutions. [PH60 Rev.3](https://github.com/ph-design/PH60) documents a dedicated removable battery tray and a much larger battery allowance; that tray concept informs the default recess design, while its FDM case and different PCB/firmware do not prove YETI60 fit or runtime.
