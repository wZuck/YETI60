# YETI60 materials and procurement requirements

Revision P2, 2026-10-28. This table is a prototype procurement plan; rows with unqualified dimensions or candidate MPNs are not approved purchase orders. Quantities are per keyboard, without spares. Electronics use the current schematic, not a historical EC60 BOM.

## Electronics

The [grouped mainboard CSV](../../hardware/bom/mainboard.csv) is regenerated by `python3 scripts/export_yeti60_bom.py`: **76 fitted components**, one DNP gain-adjustment site JP1, and no purchasable electrode, mounting-hole or SWD-pad parts. JP1 must remain open; R16/R17 already provide gain 101. There are 74 alternate-layout electrode footprints but **60 installed key mechanisms** in this HHKB plate. J3 is a six-pad SWD probe interface, not a soldered header.

| Assembly | Quantity | Requirements |
|---|---:|---|
| Main PCB | 1 | Current EC60-nRF52840 source, 4 layers, 1.59 mm finished; asymmetric HDI stack and filled/capped vias per fabrication notes |
| Mainboard components | 76 | Grouped CSV lists values, references, packages, specified/candidate MPNs and qualification gaps |
| USB daughterboard | 1 assembled | Unified Daughterboard C5 JSH, pinned commit `2b6c52a`; [upstream BOM](../../hardware/usb-daughterboard/upstream/production/UDB-C-JSH_5.0_bom.csv) has 7 fitted components; do not also count them as loose parts if buying assembled |
| USB internal harness | 1 | 150 mm preliminary wire-exit length; 4 conductors, SH-compatible housings at both ends; continuity 1→1 VBUS, 2→2 D−, 3→3 D+, 4→4 GND; plug/bend routing remains to be qualified |
| Battery pack | 1 | Default YETI60-BAT-1600-1S, HCP404074 reference, complete 85×43×4.8 mm; independent thermal interruption within actual cell 15…43°C, separate 10k NTC; PHR-3: 1 positive, 2 negative, 3 NTC. Supplier-signed limits/drawing required |
| USB-C data cable | 1 | External host cable supporting data, not charge-only |

The main J1 footprint has an inherited 1.0T-4P supplier identifier. JST SM04B-SRSS-TB is a candidate with a matching nominal interface; check retention pads and mating orientation against the [JST SH drawing](https://www.jst-mfg.com/product/pdf/eng/eSH.pdf) before ordering. J2 is S3B-PH-SM4-TB; use the [PH series drawing](https://www.jst-mfg.com/product/pdf/eng/ePH.pdf). Connector family names alone do not establish polarity. Passive MPNs, capacitance under DC bias, fuse trip behavior and the LED are not supplier-qualified; the CSV makes these gaps explicit.

## Tools and assembly release

Use an SWD probe plus the custom J3 pogo fixture, multimeter for every harness conductor/polarity, small torque-controlled driver, calipers and an insulated tool for SW100 access. No soldered per-key diodes, MX sockets or second MCU belong in this design. Quantities do not imply supplier availability or prices.

FELK60 supplies the plate/gasket mounting basis. [DELTA OMEGA BOM](https://github.com/wZuck/delta-omega/blob/main/docs/BOM.md) was reviewed at commit `64db818`: its separate mechanical/electronic rows and explicit cap envelope are useful documentation references; its MX switches, XIAO controllers and two batteries are not compatible substitutions. [PH60 Rev.3](https://github.com/ph-design/PH60) documents a dedicated removable battery tray and a much larger battery allowance; that tray concept informs the default recess design, while its FDM case and different PCB/firmware do not prove YETI60 fit or runtime.
